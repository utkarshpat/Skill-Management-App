# FDD reporting increment

The new commit `99e5c4a` contains the previously reviewed report authorization, CSV, chart semantics, scoped administration CSS and workspace timestamp corrections. Its diff and regression coverage were reviewed before this increment. No additional regression was identified in that commit. The following changes address three reporting gaps from the draft SCMD FDD; they do not claim completion of the entire document.

## Implemented reporting behavior

- **Full skill coverage:** migration 083 replaces top-10 coverage with the filtered published catalogue using the approved four-level framework. All selected ranks are returned even with zero reviewed holders. Retired skills and incompatible historical frameworks are not silently interpreted as current catalogue proficiency. Overview remains a compact top-five summary; the Skill coverage view and CSV/Excel contain the full filtered catalogue.
- **Certification-wise distinct headcount:** active credential definitions/providers have distinct reviewed, currently valid and expired employee counts. Current records use the approved-renewal projection. Counts are calculated from credentials, not multiplied by linked skills. Uncatalogued or retired credentials are separately visible and exported rather than lost or assigned an invented catalogue identity. Counts overlap across credentials and cannot be summed as workforce headcount.
- **Global ranking:** People & records offers Most current credentials. SQL counts distinct normalized issuer/name families and computes dense ranks over the matching authorized population before sorting/pagination. Ties share a rank; name and employee ID provide stable ordering. People with no current credentials have no certification rank. CSV/Excel export the complete filtered order, not the visible page.

The associated-skill filter now constrains credential reads through published links and the selected skill claims. This corrects an existing filter mismatch where a selected skill filtered skill claims but left credential headcount unchanged. Credential name/provider matching uses normalized business identity, not AI labels or proof of issuer ownership. Reporting metadata and the existing AI analytics tool expose the complete-catalogue flag and independent credential counts; old environments report unavailable coverage rather than inventing zeros.

## Authorization and performance

Migration 083 preserves the existing runtime-account binding, current canonical business projection, active workforce, usable actor-bound scope selection, matching scoped DENY, private-draft exclusion, transaction and read-time scope recheck. It introduces no grants, role assignments, person edits or review authority. Explicit null/array/foreign scope selectors fail closed.

CSV/Excel reuse export-mode SQL and the independent current export capability/revision recheck. Aggregate reports omit employee rows and export every coverage cell/credential group, including zero-holder catalogue entries. Existing row export limits remain in force; no truncated full-report success is claimed. Temporary normalized credential identity columns and indexed skill/credential aggregates avoid repeatedly normalizing all credential records for every catalogue definition.

Validity is an as-of calendar measure. Activity date filters do not turn current coverage into historical population snapshots. Review status controls record views; approved-holder charts always state their approved/current criterion. Category, issuer and linked-skill predicates apply to the relevant record type. No allocation/availability decision is implied by headcount or ranking.

## Remaining FDD work

The next reporting gap is **certification-specific monthly growth** with explicit achieved/new-current/net-growth and renewal semantics. Mixed submission/review activity must retain its current truthful label until that measure exists. Monthly lead summaries, registration/exception notifications and the below-70 alert need explicit recipient and scope policies; they are not established by role titles. Canonical Practice/Pillar/Location/certification-level dimensions require approved source data. Corporate SSO/directory synchronization, company hosting and durable scheduler activation remain deferred as requested. Oracle CertView/My Learn and governed legacy repository import remain separate integrations.
