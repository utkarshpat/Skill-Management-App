# Portfolio/Division scopes and hierarchy filters

User-approved follow-up to e37df62: Business Analytics may be explicitly assigned at Delivery Unit, Portfolio/Division or Accounts/Clients level. Generic role scopes, System Admin, organizational Project placement, Business Project membership and direct-manager review policy remain distinct.

Migration 089 adds explicit Portfolio Business responsibilities and demand bindings. Active parent paths are mandatory. `BusinessScopeCovers`, population resolution and matching DENY are shared by discovery and execution. A DU DENY overrides a Portfolio ALLOW; a client DENY removes its employees from Portfolio/DU/Organization results. Portfolio ancestry does not imply membership in separately stored Business Projects. No grants or mappings are created by migration.

Analytics offers an organization hierarchy filter grouped by Delivery Unit, Portfolio/Division and Accounts/Clients, with complete path labels. `scopeId` identifies an assigned responsibility; `hierarchyId` identifies a narrowing organizational placement. SQL intersects the hierarchy population with the canonical authorized population before aggregate counts, pagination and export. Conversational people matching uses the same optional filter. Unauthorized/foreign/inactive IDs fail closed, and changing a filter resets pagination. Administrative binding choices show parent names; Portfolio grant edits keep the existing Preview/Recheck/Save/Audit flow.
